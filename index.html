COMPUTATIONAL DESIGN

Designed parametrically. Built precisely.

We engineer building envelopes algorithmically. Every panel, every fixing, every load path — defined by parameters, optimized by simulation, validated by physical test. The result: façades that perform beyond specification, delivered on schedule even at impossible geometric complexity.

01

Complex geometry

Curved, faceted, and irregular envelopes. Parametric panel generation that conventional drafting cannot deliver within budget.

02

Material optimization

Algorithmic nesting and panel sizing that reduces waste by 15–25% on typical projects.

03

Performance simulation

Structural analysis, thermal modeling, wind load testing — all parametrically linked to the design model.
